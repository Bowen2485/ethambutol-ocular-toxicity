## 一、投稿信（英文，可直接使用）

```
Bowen Shi, Jing Yan, Zhengzheng Wu, Siyuan Yang, Yaqin Tan, Xuan Wang
Eye Hospital, China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences
33 Lugu Road, Shijingshan District, Beijing 100040, China

[Date]

The Editor-in-Chief
[Journal name]

Dear Editor,

We submit for your consideration the manuscript entitled "Ocular toxicity of
ethambutol: a whole-database pharmacovigilance analysis of 20.3 million FAERS
cases with network toxicology, molecular docking and genetic interrogation".

Ethambutol is a first-line antitubercular drug whose optic neuropathy is
irreversible in a substantial minority of those affected, and it is therefore a
toxicity where the question is not whether it exists but whether the reported
signal can be distinguished from the reporting behaviour of a tuberculosis
population. We designed the study around that question.

What we did, and what we found

We ingested all 90 FDA FAERS quarterly releases and de-duplicated 24,389,966
submitted reports to 20,328,568 unique cases. Among 13,804 de-duplicated
ethambutol cases, 971 (7.03%) reported an ocular event and 475 (3.44%) an
optic-nerve event. Of 152 ocular terms tested, 45 met strict signal criteria.

Four features distinguish this from a conventional disproportionality study:

1. An internal negative control. Not one non-specific ocular complaint formed a
   signal in any comparison - vision blurred 1.15, cataract 0.81, dry eye 0.35,
   eye pain 0.32, glaucoma 0.18, ocular hyperaemia 0.18 - while optic neuropathy
   reached an ROR of 159.21 (95% CI 134.65-188.25). A reporting artefact would
   not spare every vague symptom and single out one diagnosis.

2. The comparator is the same regimen, not the whole database. Because
   antitubercular drugs are co-prescribed, the whole-database ratio is confounded
   by the underlying disease. Stratifying by regimen, optic-nerve events occurred
   in 67 of 5,757 ethambutol-containing regimens (1.16%) against 3 of 845
   regimens without it (0.36%) - a rate ratio of 3.28 (95% CI 1.03-10.40). The
   within-class ratio is 6.47 (95% CI 5.38-7.79; 171 cases), and it survives a
   denominator restricted to anti-tuberculosis reports (ROR 7.64, 95% CI
   5.24-11.13).

3. External replication in a different national system. The pattern reproduces in
   JADER, Japan's spontaneous reporting database, where optic neuropathy gives an
   ROR of 571 (95% CI 414-788) and toxic optic neuropathy 865 (95% CI
   426-1753), with cataract (1.40) and glaucoma (1.26) null. The magnitudes are
   not comparable between the two systems and we say so; the pattern replicates.

4. We report what the data cannot support. Only 27.8% of the optic-nerve cases
   carry enough detail for time-to-onset analysis, so we present the temporal
   result as supporting rather than primary. Daily dose does not predict optic
   outcome (likelihood-ratio P = 0.55), and we report that null. A genetic
   interrogation found no causal effect of renal function on any optic-nerve
   phenotype (151-186 harmonised instruments per outcome across three renal
   measures; all q >= 0.87), which we interpret as locating renal impairment in
   exposure rather than in causation - an informative negative rather than a
   failed analysis.

The mechanism component is presented as hypothesis generation. Ethambutol has no
recorded human target in Open Targets, so the network we built is a downstream
effect network rather than a target network; its enrichment converges on
ferroptosis and mitochondrial integrity, which agrees with independent
experimental work on SDHB and Smad4. Single-cell retinal expression data show 31
of the 33 network hubs expressed in retinal clusters (empirical P = 0.0067
against random gene sets of the same size), with the ferroptosis machinery -
GPX4, SLC7A11, ACSL4, SDHB - abundantly represented. Docking ranked four hubs
highest at affinities of only -4.95 to -5.50 kcal/mol, and all-atom molecular
dynamics in three of those sites kept the ligand in contact with the protein
throughout the simulated window.

Why we believe it suits your journal

[Choose one, and delete the rest before sending]

  Frontiers in Pharmacology       The journal publishes the combination of
                                  pharmacovigilance signal detection, network
                                  toxicology and docking that this paper uses,
                                  and does not require wet-lab confirmation of a
                                  hypothesis-generating mechanism.
  BMC Ophthalmology               Ocular adverse events are squarely within its
                                  scope, and the negative-control design speaks
                                  to the specificity question its readership
                                  asks about drug-induced optic neuropathy.
  Drug Safety                     The methodological requirements of this
                                  journal match what we did: four pre-specified
                                  robustness specifications, whole-database
                                  de-duplication by the FDA case-version rule,
                                  and an explicit account of report adequacy.
  Frontiers in Neurology          The optic-nerve phenotype is the paper's centre
                                  and the neuro-ophthalmology readership is the
                                  audience that would act on it.

Statements

All data are from public sources (FDA FAERS, JADER, Open Targets, STRING,
g:Profiler, Human Protein Atlas, FinnGen, OpenGWAS, PubChem, RCSB PDB). No
patient-identifiable information was accessed and no ethics approval was
required. The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and
all authors have approved its submission. We declare no competing interests. The
analysis code, derived tables and figures are openly available, and the
repository URL is given in the manuscript.

Suggested reviewers, if the journal requires them, are not listed here because we
would rather the editor select them; we can provide names on request.

Yours sincerely,

Jing Yan
Eye Hospital, China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences

## 二、投稿信（中文对照）

```
施博文、闫晶、吴铮铮、杨思远、谭亚琴、王璇
中国中医科学院眼科医院
北京市石景山区鲁谷路 33 号，100040

[日期]

[期刊名称] 主编

尊敬的编辑：

现提交研究论文《乙胺丁醇的眼毒性：2 033 万例 FAERS 全库药物警戒分析结合网络毒理学、
分子对接与遗传学考察》，敬请审阅。

乙胺丁醇是一线抗结核药物，其视神经病变在相当一部分患者中不可逆。因此该毒性的关键
问题不是"是否存在"，而是"所报信号能否与结核病人群自身的报告行为相区分"。本研究
即围绕这一问题设计。

研究内容与发现

我们纳入了全部 90 个 FDA FAERS 季度发布包，按 FDA 病例版本规则将 24 389 966 份
提交报告去重为 20 328 568 例唯一病例。在 13 804 例去重乙胺丁醇病例中，971 例
（7.03%）报告了眼部事件，475 例（3.44%）报告了视神经事件。152 个受检眼部术语中
45 个符合严格信号标准。

以下四点使本研究区别于常规的不成比例分析：

一、内部阴性对照。在任何比较中，没有任何非特异性眼部主诉构成信号——视物模糊 1.15、
白内障 0.81、干眼 0.35、眼痛 0.32、青光眼 0.18、眼部充血 0.18——而视神经病变的
ROR 达 159.21（95%CI 134.65–188.25）。报告偏倚不会放过所有含糊症状而单独挑出一个
诊断。

二、对照是同一方案，而非全库。由于抗结核药联合处方，全库比值受基础疾病混杂。按方案
分层后，含乙胺丁醇方案中视神经事件为 67/5 757（1.16%），不含者为 3/845（0.36%），
率比 3.28（95%CI 1.03–10.40）。类内比值 6.47（95%CI 5.38–7.79；171 例），且在
分母限定为抗结核报告时仍然成立（ROR 7.64，95%CI 5.24–11.13）。

三、在另一国家系统中外部复现。该模式在日本 JADER 中复现：视神经病变 ROR 571
（95%CI 414–788），中毒性视神经病变 865（95%CI 426–1753），而白内障（1.40）与
青光眼（1.26）无信号。两系统的数值量级不可直接比较，我们已明确说明；复现的是模式。

四、我们报告数据无法支持的部分。仅 27.8% 的视神经病例具备足够细节做发病时间分析，
故时序结果作为支持性而非主要证据呈现。日剂量不预测视神经结局（似然比检验 P = 0.55），
该阴性结果已报告。遗传学考察未发现肾功能对任何视神经表型的因果效应（三个肾功能指标
各覆盖每个结局 151–186 个协调工具变量；全部 q ≥ 0.87），我们将其解读为把肾功能不全
定位在暴露环节而非因果环节——这是有信息量的阴性结果，而非失败的分析。

机制部分作为假设生成呈现。乙胺丁醇在 Open Targets 中无人类靶点记录，故所建网络是
下游效应网络而非靶点网络；其富集收敛于铁死亡与线粒体完整性，与 SDHB、Smad4 的独立
实验研究一致。单细胞视网膜表达数据显示 33 个网络 hub 中 31 个在视网膜细胞簇中表达
（对同规模随机基因集的经验 P = 0.0067），铁死亡机器——GPX4、SLC7A11、ACSL4、SDHB
——高表达。对接将四个 hub 排在前列，但亲和力仅 −4.95 至 −5.50 kcal/mol；在这四个
位点中的三个所做的全原子分子动力学在整个模拟窗口内保持配体与蛋白接触。
