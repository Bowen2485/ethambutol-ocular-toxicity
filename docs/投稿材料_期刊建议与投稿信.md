## 一、期刊建议

### 1.1 英文 SCI 版

**首选：《Frontiers in Pharmacology》— 栏目 *Pharmacology of Anti-infective Drugs* 或 *Pharmacoepidemiology***

| 维度 | 评估 |
|---|---|
| IF | 约 4.4（近年版） |
| 契合度 | 该刊大量发表 FAERS 信号挖掘＋网络药理学＋分子对接的组合型论文，形态完全匹配 |
| 接收门槛 | 对纯计算研究友好；不要求湿实验 |
| 周转 | 审稿通常 6–10 周；接受后 2–4 周上线 |
| 风险 | 需支付 APC（约 3 000–3 500 USD）；须在投稿信中说明经费来源 |
| 建议 | **若经费允许，此为最优选** |

**次选：《Drug Design, Development and Therapy》（Dove Medical Press）**

| 维度 | 评估 |
|---|---|
| IF | 约 4.7 |
| 契合度 | 接受药物警戒＋机制＋对接的组合，篇幅容忍度高 |
| 周转 | 较快 |
| 风险 | 同样有 APC |

**备选：《Frontiers in Neurology》— 栏目 *Neuro-Ophthalmology***

| 维度 | 评估 |
|---|---|
| IF | 约 2.7 |
| 契合度 | 视神经病变的临床与机制研究归属明确；对中医讨论部分接受度较高 |
| 优势 | IF 门槛适中，与本研究"药物警戒＋机制"的定位相符 |
| 建议 | **若追求稳妥与低 APC，此为首选** |

**跳一跳（风险较高，不建议首投）**
- *British Journal of Ophthalmology*：会要求更强的临床队列证据，纯 FAERS 分析难达其门槛
- *Drug Safety*：对自发报告方法学要求极严，且偏好方法学创新而非应用型信号挖掘

### 1.2 中文核心版

**首选：《中国中医眼科杂志》— 栏目"临床研究"或"学术探讨"**

- 该刊为中医眼科领域唯一专业核心期刊，与本文"中医病机＋现代数据"的定位最契合
- 论著篇幅通常 ≤6 000 字，需按此压缩（当前中文版约 8 000 字，需精简约 25%）
- 无基金门槛，答复约 3 个月

**次选：《中国药房》或《药物流行病学杂志》**

- 若强调药物警戒部分，可投《药物流行病学杂志》；该刊对 FAERS 类研究有明确栏目
- 中医部分需大幅压缩至讨论段

**备选：《中华中医药杂志》或《中国中药杂志》**

- 若强调"中药干预中毒性视神经病变"的假设，可投《中国中药杂志》
- 但需注意：本文中药部分为计算推演，无实验验证，可能被要求补充数据

### 1.3 双版本策略建议

考虑到你 2027 年 12 月前需见刊 2 篇科技核心的硬指标：

1. **中文版先行**（投《中国中医眼科杂志》），因周转可预期、无 APC
2. **英文版同步投出**（投《Frontiers in Neurology》或《Frontiers in Pharmacology》）
3. 两版内容差异化：中文版突出中医病机与治法；英文版突出药物警戒方法学与机制计算
4. **避免同刊同期多篇**：若《中国中医眼科杂志》已有其他在投稿件，本文改投《药物流行病学杂志》

## 二、投稿信（英文版）

Dear Editor,

We submit for your consideration our manuscript entitled **"Ocular toxicity of ethambutol: a whole-database pharmacovigilance analysis of 20.3 million FAERS cases integrated with network toxicology, molecular docking and genetic interrogation of the optic-nerve phenotype"** for publication as an Original Research article.

**What is new.** Ethambutol-induced optic neuropathy is the toxicity that most limits the use of a first-line antitubercular drug, yet its reported frequency spans an order of magnitude (0.28%–6.5%) and no study has profiled its complete ocular phenotype against the other first-line antitubercular drugs across the whole FAERS database with formal multiplicity control. We addressed this by ingesting all 90 FDA FAERS quarterly ASCII releases, de-duplicating 24.4 million submitted reports to 20.3 million unique cases using the FDA case-version protocol, and testing 73 ocular MedDRA preferred terms across six antitubercular drugs.

**The three findings we believe will interest your readership.**

1. *A signal of unusual specificity.* Optic neuropathy returned a reporting odds ratio of 159.21 (95% CI 134.65–188.25) with an IC025 of 6.58 — an order of magnitude above any comparator drug — while non-specific ocular complaints produced **no** signal at all (vision blurred 1.82; cataract 0.86; dry eye 0.42; eye pain 0.64; glaucoma 0.38). That internal contrast is what makes the finding resistant to the usual reporting-bias objection.

2. *A timing signature compatible with cumulative axonal injury.* Median time from starting ethambutol to an optic-nerve event was 125 days (IQR 63–293) versus 30 days (IQR 9–85) for non-ocular events in the same reports.

3. *A mechanism that converges on ferroptosis.* Because Open Targets holds **no human target record** for ethambutol (it inhibits mycobacterial arabinosyltransferases), we reconstructed the human gene set from a full-text mine of 3,034 Europe PMC records. Enrichment converged on ferroptosis (KEGG, *P* = 6.1 × 10⁻⁷) — the exact axis identified experimentally in ethambutol-treated retinal ganglion cells — with mitochondrion, oxidative-stress and BDNF–NTRK2 signals alongside.

We also report molecular docking of ethambutol and nine traditional Chinese medicine actives against the hub panel, and a traditional Chinese medicine mechanistic section that maps the classical *failure of clear yang to ascend* reading of toxic optic neuropathy onto the mitochondrial and lipid-peroxidative findings.

**Methodological transparency.** We document several things that would otherwise be buried: that sustained bulk retrieval from openFDA is throttled, so we used the FDA's own quarterly ASCII releases; that one quarterly archive (2026Q2) is corrupt and was excluded; that 42 of 168 docking runs failed for large flexible ligands; that the dihydrofolate reductase redocking control failed (RMSD 5.86 Å) while the carbonic anhydrase control passed (1.79 Å); and that a drug-exposure Mendelian randomisation is not possible for this drug, with the reasons stated in full.

**Declarations.** The manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved the submission. All data analysed are publicly available and de-identified; no ethics approval was required. [Insert funding and competing-interest statements.]

We hope you find the work suitable for [Journal name] and look forward to your assessment.

## 三、投稿信（中文版）

尊敬的编辑：

现向贵刊投送我们的研究论文《乙胺丁醇眼毒性：2033 万例 FAERS 全库药物警戒分析及"清阳不升"病机的计算验证》，申请以论著形式发表。

**本研究的创新点。** 乙胺丁醇视神经毒性是限制这一一线抗结核药使用的主要毒性，但其报告发生率跨度达一个数量级（0.28%～6.5%），且尚无研究在 FAERS 全库范围内、以术语级信号挖掘并设正式多重检验校正的方式，将其完整眼部表型与同类抗结核药对照。本研究摄取 FDA FAERS 全部 90 个季度 ASCII 原始发布，按 FDA 病例版本规则将 2 439 万份提交记录去重为 2 033 万例唯一病例，对 73 个眼科 MedDRA 首选术语在 6 种抗结核药间做系统比较。

**我们认为最值得关注的三个结果。**

其一，**信号特异性极高**。视神经病变报告比值比达 159.21（95%CI 134.65～188.25），IC025 为 6.58，高于任何对照药一个量级；而泛化眼部主诉**完全无信号**（视物模糊 1.82、白内障 0.86、干眼 0.42、眼痛 0.64、青光眼 0.38）。这一内部对照是本研究区别于一般信号挖掘的关键，也是排除报告偏倚的有力证据。

其二，**时序符合累积性轴索损伤**。用药至视神经事件的中位时间为 125 d（IQR 63～293），而同一批报告中非眼部事件仅 30 d（IQR 9～85）。

其三，**机制收敛于铁死亡**。因 Open Targets 中乙胺丁醇**无人类靶点记录**（其作用靶点为分枝杆菌阿拉伯糖基转移酶），我们依据 3 034 篇 Europe PMC 文献全文挖掘重建人类基因集，富集首位即为 KEGG 铁死亡通路（P=6.1×10⁻⁷）——与最新实验研究在乙胺丁醇处理视网膜神经节细胞中发现的通路完全一致，同时伴线粒体、氧化应激与 BDNF-NTRK2 信号。

**中医部分。** 本研究为"清阳不升、浊阴壅络"病机提供了可计算的分子锚点：清阳之升在细胞层面即线粒体产能与轴浆运输，"浊阴"可对应脂质过氧化负荷。据此提出益气升阳、化瘀解毒、益精养神三法，并以分子对接给出候选方药的优先验证顺序。**我们明确声明：未检索到任何以乙胺丁醇视神经毒性为模型评价中药的研究，所有方药建议均为待验证假设，不构成临床用药建议。**

**方法学透明度。** 本文如实记录了若干通常被省略的细节：openFDA 批量取数受限流拦截故改用 FDA 官方季度原始发布；2026Q2 压缩包损坏已排除；168 次对接中 42 次因大分子 PDBQT 转换失败；二氢叶酸还原酶回对接未通过（RMSD 5.86 Å）而碳酸酐酶回对接通过（1.79 Å）；乙胺丁醇不存在遗传工具变量，故未行药物暴露的孟德尔随机化。

## 四、数据与代码可用性声明（可直接使用）

**Data availability.** All data analysed in this study are publicly available.
FAERS quarterly ASCII releases were obtained from the U.S. Food and Drug
Administration (`https://fis.fda.gov/extensions/FPD-QDE-FAERS/FPD-QDE-FAERS.html`).
Drug mechanism and disease-association data were retrieved from Open Targets
(GraphQL API v4), protein–protein interactions from STRING v12, functional
enrichment from g:Profiler, compound structures from PubChem, experimental
structures from the RCSB Protein Data Bank with AlphaFold models where no
experimental structure exists, and genetic instruments from OpenGWAS. FinnGen
release 12 summary statistics were accessed through the public bucket
(`https://storage.googleapis.com/finngen-public-data-r12`). No new data were
generated.

**Code availability.** All retrieval, processing and analysis steps are
implemented as standalone, re-runnable Python scripts and are available at
[repository URL to be inserted]. The pipeline covers FAERS ingestion and
case-version de-duplication, disproportionality statistics, case-level and
time-to-onset analysis, literature target mining, network construction and
enrichment, blind pocket detection, docking with a redocking validation harness,
a minimal BGZF/tabix reader for on-demand access to FinnGen summary statistics,
and two-sample Mendelian randomisation.

**Ethics.** Not required. FAERS contains de-identified, voluntarily submitted
adverse event reports; no human participants were recruited and no identifiable
information was handled.
